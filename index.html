AB206 REALSIM - Bell 206B-3

Educational simulation, not a training device. Turn the phone to landscape. Left strip is the collective, the bottom slider is the pedals, the round pad on the right is the cyclic. Starts at Khouribga (GMMK).

  • Keyboard and mouse also work on a computer (F1 for controls).
  • MAP opens the moving map (tap again for full screen), PIN drops a point where you are, INSTR zooms on the instruments, MAP +/- zooms the small map.
  • Hard with one hand? The PEDALS button switches to AUTO (the pedals are flown for you) or TILT (turn the phone like a steering wheel). On iPhone allow Motion when asked.
  • Graphics start on Ultra on iPhone (MENU, Settings to change). Real 3D terrain covers about 100 km around Khouribga, including the mine pits. A real HD aerial photo streams onto the ground while you fly, when your connection allows it.
  • Sound starts after you tap Start. MENU switches map, lessons and missions.
